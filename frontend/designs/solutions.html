Capabilities

What The Voice Agent Can Do

A single real-time pipeline — not a bundle of separate products.

Full-duplex conversation

Talk and listen at the same time, like a real phone call — no walkie-talkie turn-taking, no waiting for a reply to finish rendering.

Live transcription

Every word is transcribed in real time, for both sides of the conversation, visible during and after the call.

Low-latency voice pipeline

Audio streams continuously in and out, so replies start almost as soon as a person stops talking.

Configurable agent persona

The same pipeline can be pointed at a different role or script — receptionist, support line, intake assistant — through configuration, not a rebuild.

Browser-native access

Runs entirely over WebRTC in the browser. No app download, no special hardware, no phone line needed to try it.

Self-hosted architecture

Built on open infrastructure — WebRTC transport, a self-hosted voice model — rather than a closed third-party black box.
